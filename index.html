CGC ERP User Guide

1. Easy Login

Apna Login Code small ya capital mein dalein (bina dash ke bhi chalega, jaise dg01 ya blr01) aur PIN daalkar login karein.

2. DG Sets Portal (Diesel & HMR)

Tank card par "Diesel Rate" click karke rate badal sakte hain aur "+ DG Entry" mein Start/Stop HMR aur diesel liters log kar sakte hain.

3. Base Kitchen Steam Boiler (LPG Gas)

Base kitchen ka steam boiler gas par chalta hai—iske liye alag Boiler tab hai jahan shift, steam pressure (PSI) aur gas cylinders ka stock track hota hai.

4. Smart QR Checklist (No Typing!)

Machine par lage QR code ko scan karte hi uski checklist checkboxes ke sath khul jayegi—bas completed kaam par tick lagayein aur Save dabayein.
